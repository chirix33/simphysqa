The source imbalance follows admitted question counts and physical attempts:6,800attempted scenarios,6,340admitted,460rejected. Driving has1,279predictive QA, versus3,226arm and3,300each pushing/tethered; source manifests show partition-specific admission. Later uneven counts follow engineering history: per domain4direct+4predictive+2each counterfactual/explanation/clarification, then7each for audit and20each for held-out preparation. Thus31direct/predictive and29each later family per domain. Later families require verified matched outcomes, explicit supplied comparison or scorer-disjoint witnesses. These are construction constraints, not quality-selected natural frequencies. SPQ06 preserves15clarification-candidate rejections and same-cell reserves; no criterion was weakened.

Future expansion could freeze another source/order and budget, qualify more outcomes or more genuine gaps. It is not executed and cannot be counted. Adding/relabeling source items to fill paper tables would change the population and is outside this confirmation.
