required computation (if any) and exact answer. No future simulation is required
when the observed input determines the answer.
PREDICTIVE: identify initial/public conditions, effective model/control, actual
saved numerical simulation/settings/run, requested future readout and verification.
Explain supported physical facts/state changes, beyond repeating the gold value.
COUNTERFACTUAL: identify precise omission, unchanged conditions, matched factual/
alternative runs and readouts, supported comparison and remaining limitations.

implementation/model interpretation separately from historical frame metadata.

maximum motor limit is not realized torque. Objects remove external force on

program including positive/negative acceleration and subsequent displacement.
